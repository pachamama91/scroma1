About the Club

ROMA SC (Rongena/Manaret Sporting Club) is a premier athletic organization dedicated to fostering talent and community spirit. Founded on the principles of excellence and discipline, we aim to unite the Rongena and Manaret regions through the universal language of sports.

Club History

Established in 2026, ROMA SC was born from a vision to create a professional platform for local athletes. Despite being a young club, our foundations are built on decades of local sporting passion, aiming to become a powerhouse in regional competitions.
